Questions, answered plainly.

Still wondering about something? Ask a person, we reply within a day.

Who can use ShopTalk?

Retail, service and hybrid small businesses. Onboarding adapts the catalogue and modules to your type.

Can the assistant record a wrong sale?

It cannot save anything alone. Every consequential action is previewed with backend prices and waits for your confirmation; duplicates replay safely.

How accurate are the figures?

Reports are calculated by deterministic backend code from your saved records, totals, stock and profit are never model-generated.

Which countries and currencies work?

All 249 countries with their currencies and IANA timezones, via open ISO data. Change currency any time in Setup.

Are my records private?

Every record is scoped to your business. Other businesses get a 404, and role permissions are enforced on the server for every request.

Do I need an internet connection?

Saving needs a live backend. ShopTalk says so clearly when you are offline instead of failing silently, and typed requests work wherever voice does not.

Can my team use it?

Yes, owner, manager, sales, inventory, finance and viewer roles, each bounded by the backend, with an audit trail of who did what.
